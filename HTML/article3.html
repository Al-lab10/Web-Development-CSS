Meta Glasses Get New AI Capabilities

by Suyou Xi · October 5, 2026

Meta's smart glasses are getting a new set of artificial intelligence features, aimed at making the glasses more helpful in everyday situations. The update is part of the company's wider push into wearable technology, an area that Meta co-founder Mark Zuckerberg has often described as a key part of the future of computing. It shows how wearable devices are moving beyond simple notifications and becoming more interactive and aware of their surroundings.

One of the main additions is an improved voice assistant. Users can ask questions, set reminders, or get quick information hands-free, without taking out their phones. The assistant is designed to understand natural speech, so people can talk to the glasses the way they would to another person.

Another highlighted feature is visual recognition. Using the built-in camera, the glasses can help identify objects, read text on signs or menus, and give users details about what they are looking at. This can be useful for travelers, students, and anyone who wants quick information about the world around them.

The update also improves communication features. The glasses can read incoming messages aloud and let users reply by voice. Live translation is another area of development, with the goal of helping people understand conversations in different languages while on the go.

Privacy remains an important topic for smart glasses. Because the devices include cameras and microphones, the company says it is adding clearer indicators and controls so that both users and the people around them know when recording or recognition is active.
